Peng Liu is a professor at Cornell University’s SC Johnson College of Business and the Nolan School of Hotel Administration. His research examines the interaction between financial markets and the real economy, including the valuation and financing of commercial real estate and commodities, hospitality markets, securitization, and REITs. Before joining academia, he worked at Goldman Sachs Asset Management and Deloitte Consulting. He earned a Ph.D. in real estate and finance and an M.S. in finance from UC Berkeley, an M.A. in financial economics from Peking University, and a B.S. in engineering from Tsinghua University.
