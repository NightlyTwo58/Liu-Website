About

I study how financial markets interact with the real economy, with a focus on the valuation and financing of real assets: commercial real estate, hotels, REITs, securitized debt, and commodities.

I am a full professor of real estate and finance at Cornell SC Johnson College of Business. I have held the Singapore Tourism Board Distinguished Professorship in Asian Hospitality Management at the Nolan School of Hotel Administration since 2016 and became an inaugural faculty member of the Paul Rubacha Department of Real Estate in 2023. Before Cornell, I was a research fellow at UC Berkeley’s Fisher Center for Real Estate and Urban Economics (2002–2007), and worked at Goldman Sachs Asset Management in New York and Deloitte Consulting in Beijing.
