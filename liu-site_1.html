About

Peng (Peter) Liu is Professor of Real Estate and Finance at Cornell’s Nolan School of Hotel Administration and an inaugural faculty member of the Rubacha Department of Real Estate. His research examines how financial markets interact with the real economy, with a focus on the valuation and financing of real assets such as commercial real estate, hotels, and commodities.

He directs the Cornell–Peking University MMH/MBA dual degree, serves as Faculty Research Director of the Cornell SC Johnson Center for Real Estate and Finance, and edits the Journal of Real Estate Portfolio Management. Before joining Cornell in 2007 he was a research fellow at UC Berkeley’s Fisher Center for Real Estate and Urban Economics, and he has worked at Goldman Sachs Asset Management and Deloitte Consulting. He holds a Ph.D. from UC Berkeley Haas, an M.A. from Peking University, and a B.S. from Tsinghua University.
