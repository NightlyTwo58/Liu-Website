He joined Cornell in 2007 and became an inaugural faculty member of the Paul Rubacha Department of Real Estate in 2022. His research examines real asset valuation and financing, including commercial real estate, REITs, securitization, commodity pricing, and hospitality markets. Before academia, he worked at Goldman Sachs Asset Management in New York and Deloitte Consulting in Beijing.
